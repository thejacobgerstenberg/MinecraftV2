Toast

Every status pairs its color with a required glyph — info=droplet, success=stitch-check, warn=fray-knot triangle, error=frayed-X — plus a colored left hem (position cue). Live toasts spawn via <lf-toast-rack>.show(): fixed top-right, max 3 visible, ~5s auto-dismiss paused on hover/focus. Demos are sticky (no duration).

The cloth that dreams it is a world.

A voxel survival game woven from thread. The maker is gone, the great Loom turns on with no hand on the shuttle — and you are the one loose thread that woke. Gather, build, and bind what you love before it all comes gently apart.

Nothing here is finished. That is not a flaw. That is the invitation.

Every stitch yours to pull

  • A boundless woven world

    Embroidered meadows, brocade forests, mountains of warp-thread pulled taut toward the sky. Every world is woven fresh — wander until the detail thins into sketch at the Unfinished Hem, then keep going.

  • Three realms, one thread

    One length of cloth caught at three moments of its living: the lush Warpwold above, the burning Cinderloom below, and the frayed void of Nevermend past the last selvage.

  • Weave together

    Open your world to others, or choose Join a Weaving and share their days, nights, and frays. Anchor at a friend's knotlight, bind together, and reach the ending side by side.

  • Three looks, one loom

    Every texture is spun by code — no image files, ever. Swap between the crisp default weave, a soft high-dye cartoon, and a weathered gritty cut, live, without leaving your world.

The Three Realms

Furnace over cloth over void — and a thread pulled anywhere is felt everywhere along it.

The lived-in middle

Warpwold

The tapestry grown so thick it forgot it was ever thread. Rolling embroidered grasslands, humming forests of braided cord, ghost-terrain remembering out loud where the cloth wears thin — and the courteous Wickerkin, who dream they are alive and keep the dream swept.

The burning below

Cinderloom

Where the weaving actually happens and has never been allowed to finish. Raw thread drawn shrieking through white fire, rivers of molten skein, colossal ember-spindles — and the Scaldwardens, tending an order no one ever cancelled. No shade. No night.

The finished edge

Nevermend

Islands of half-unravelled tapestry adrift in an unlit deep, each still ghosting the meadow or face it used to be. Build one stitch ahead of your feet, keep your gold thread close — it is the only warm thing here — and make for the shining rim of the Last Selvage.

How to play

You wake as a Mender — a loose thread that chooses its own direction. Here is your road, in four stitches.

  1. 1

    Survive your first day

    Pick Warpsod loose bare-handed, fell Thrumwood trees, and gather Raw Skein from grazing skeinlings to craft your Handloom. Place a Knotlight and set your anchor — if you are unpicked, you are re-stitched at your last knot. Walls and light before dusk; the dark treats any unlit corner as an invitation.

  2. 2

    Climb the thread ladder

    Tools follow one ladder: Knotwood → Threadstone → Needle-Iron → Dawnthread → Everthread. Follow the Thrum — the low hum in your teeth — toward raw material. Shear skeinlings, don't slaughter them, and trade Giltspool with the Wickerkin by daylight.

  3. 3

    Descend through a loom-gate

    Frame a gate in Cinderglass with a living fray drawn taut across it, and step down into the Cinderloom. Bring home Bindwax — binding a block makes it permanent, past hound, Raveler, or the Ravelling itself — and take Everthread from Molthkin, the First Bobbin.

  4. 4

    Cross the void, thread the Needle

    In Nevermend, build one stitch ahead of your feet — or ride a bonded spoolmare, which stitches ground beneath its own hooves. At the Eye of the Last Hem waits the Last Needle: finish the weaving in defiance of the void, or let the Loom fall silent at last. The choice is yours.

The Knotlight rule

Unpicked, never lost

Death in Loomfall is called being unpicked — and it is not the end. Set your anchor at a knotlight and the world simply re-stitches you at your last knot, boots on, lesson learned, night still waiting.

And when you find Bindwax in the deep, binding makes a build permanent — past hound, Raveler, or the Ravelling itself. Mend what you can. Bind what you love.
